# Harborfall — Story Bible

## Premise
Port Sable is a working harbour city: cranes, ferries, fish markets and
trucking yards, all washed in teal-and-amber dusk light. Five years ago a
smuggling run went wrong. **Tamim Kade** took the fall so his younger
brother could run. Now Tamim is up for early release — but only if the
"debt" recorded in harbour boss **Victor Solano's** private ledger is
settled. His brother **Rayan Kade**, a former getaway driver, comes home
to settle it — or expose it.

**Themes:** loyalty, debt, second chances. Who owes whom, really?

## Characters
- **Rayan Kade** — protagonist. Former harbour smuggler/getaway driver.
  Left Port Sable 5 years ago; returns on the night ferry with a duffel
  bag and a plan he hasn't fully admitted to himself.
- **Tamim Kade** — older brother. In prison for the run that went wrong.
  Hid three torn pages of Solano's ledger before his arrest as insurance.
- **Victor Solano** — antagonist. Owns the cranes, the trucking empire
  (Solano Trucking) and, through the ledger, half the docks. Never raises
  his voice; the ledger does it for him.
- **Detective Mara Voss** — harbour patrol detective. Honest, by-the-rules,
  has wanted Solano for years and lacks only proof. Gives the wanted
  system its face: she would rather use Rayan than arrest him — barely.
- **Lina Park** — mechanic, old friend, runs Lina's Garage on Market Row.
  The player's home base for car missions and hard truths.
- **Old Elias** — fisherman at the pier, mentor figure. Tamim mended his
  nets every winter; Elias repays debts the harbour way.

## Chapters (in-game, in order)
1. **Homecoming** (Lina) — drive Rayan's Sable Sprinter from the pier to
   Lina's garage. *Teaches driving.*
2. **First Favour** (Elias) — pick up harbour charts at the pier, deliver
   to the Ferry Quarter depot against the clock. *Teaches timed delivery
   and that speed draws the patrol.*
3. **Ledger Run** (Lina, for Tamim) — recover the 3 hidden ledger pages:
   Lantern Park, Gull Heights, East Yards.
4. **Heat on the Docks** (Rayan/Lina) — Solano tips off the patrol:
   forced 3-star wanted. Reach the Ferry Quarter safehouse — but only
   once you've lost the heat.
5. **Voss's Bargain** (Det. Voss) — drive her evidence route (crane
   office → trucking yard → union hall) with **zero** wanted stars.
6. **Solano's Last Shipment** (Voss) — finale: race the convoy route to
   the harbour gate before Solano's last shipment sails.

## Ending
The shipment is seized and the ledger published. Solano's empire is
arrested in public; Tamim walks free into the dusk. Rayan keeps the car,
Lina clears his garage tab, and Port Sable becomes just a harbour again.
The game continues in free roam with a **★ Story Complete** badge and all
repeatable jobs still available.

## Repeatable jobs (post-/inter-story)
Harbour Delivery · Checkpoint Sprint · Harbour Tokens — rotating paid
work from "Dispatch", using the same systems as the campaign.
